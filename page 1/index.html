North India

Rajasthan & Golden Triangle

6–10 Days • Oct–Mar

Key Destinations: Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer, Pushkar & Agra

Experience royal Rajput grandeur, majestic hilltop forts of Amer and Mehrangarh, tranquil Lake Pichola golden hour boat rides, desert camps in Thar, and iconic street food like Pyaaz Kachoris and Dal Baati.

Hilltop Forts Lake Pichola Thar Desert Royal Thali
South India

Kerala & Western Ghats

5–8 Days • Sep–Mar

Key Destinations: Munnar, Alleppey, Fort Kochi, Wayanad & Varkala

Sail the serene palm-fringed backwaters aboard private luxury houseboats, wander through mist-cloaked Munnar tea gardens, watch sunset at Fort Kochi's Chinese fishing nets, and relish authentic Sadhya on fresh banana leaves.

Backwaters Tea Gardens Kathakali Ayurveda
West Coast

Goa & Konkan Coast

4–7 Days • Nov–Apr

Key Destinations: Panjim, Fontainhas, South Goa Coves, Palolem & Gokarna

Discover the pastel Portuguese heritage villas of Fontainhas, secluded swims at Cola Beach's emerald lagoon, dramatic clifftop ramparts at Cabo de Rama, fragrant organic spice farms, and beachside bohemian cafes.

Latin Quarter Secret Coves Spice Farms Coastal Cafes
Himalayas

Himachal & Spiti Valleys

6–12 Days • May–Oct

Key Destinations: Kaza, Chandratal, Sangla, Chitkul, Manali & Dharamshala

Embark on legendary mountain road trips through the world's highest post office at Hikkim, 1,000-year-old cliffside Buddhist monasteries at Key & Tabo, camp beside turquoise Chandratal Lake, and cross thrilling high passes.

High Passes Chandratal Camp Ancient Gompas Stargazing
East & Northeast

Northeast Seven Sisters

7–12 Days • Oct–Apr

Key Destinations: Meghalaya (Cherrapunji, Dawki), Assam (Kaziranga), Sikkim & Tawang

Trek to the bio-engineered Double Decker Living Root Bridges, cruise crystal-clear Dawki Umngot river where boats appear floating in mid-air, spot one-horned rhinos in Kaziranga, and visit India's largest monastery in Tawang.

Root Bridges Transparent Dawki Kaziranga Rhino Kanchenjunga
